Number

A fraction like 22/7, a decimal like 2.5, or any string of digits such as a date.
Base 10 uses the familiar digits 0–9.

Melody

96 BPM

Harmony

80%

Every digit is one note. Digit 1 is the home note of the scale, 2 the next step up, and so on; 0 sits one step below home.

Chords come only from the digits: each group of digits picks its own digit as the chord root and builds a triad in the same scale. Nothing is added that the number doesn't contain.

Press Space to play or pause.
